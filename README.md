## REACH AI
AI-Powered Cold Outreach CRM for Freelancers and Consultants


ReachAI is a smart CRM platform designed to help freelancers, consultants, and solo founders streamline
their cold outreach process. It uses AI-powered email generation, automated follow-ups, and email
engagement tracking to improve response rates and reduce manual work. The tool is built for solo users
with a clean UI, fast onboarding, and integrations tailored to individual prospecting—not large sales teams.
